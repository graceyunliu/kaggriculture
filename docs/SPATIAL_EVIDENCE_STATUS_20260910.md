# Spatial hypothesis: evidence status, 2026-09-10

Spatial specialization is not an established architectural pillar. Excess travel is observed, but the tested interventions have not shown that it causes O15's economic gap. Do not treat the earlier dispatcher-rewrite plan's spatial causal narrative as confirmed.

- The 36-game O12/O15/X1 audit reproduced excess movement and separated issued from state-changing work. Its tape comparison is observational.
- The 480-game O15 placement × routing factorial found no promotion-worthy spatial/economic chain. Its compact placement changed geometry only slightly, so it does not refute all layout effects.
- The 120-trajectory frozen-state measurement fixes newly selected bundles, eligible workers, priorities and existing claims. Exact matching saves about 26 projected entry tiles/game; permitting idle crop workers adds about 0.1 tile. These are snapshot costs, not realized full-game savings or profit. Deprioritize narrow same-tier worker selection; leave workload generation, route ordering and claim lifecycle separate and unresolved.
- The follow-up route audit reconstructs successful work and movement on those 120 trajectories. Only 10.5 task-to-task excess tiles/game occur without a shed operation. Exact ordering of fully valid fixed bundles saves 6.0 projected tiles with both endpoints fixed, 10.3 with the endpoint released, or 16.7 with head and endpoint released within original task-kind slots. Compatible bypasses are observable, but strict passed-near-and-returned cases average 5.7/game. Deprioritize within-bundle ordering; workload generation, bundle formation, task timing, claim lifecycle and necessary logistics remain unresolved.

No roles, economic rules, policy packages or submissions were changed. Prior coordination, lifecycle and product-flow results remain separate historical evidence; they were not re-estimated in this study.
